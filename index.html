04 · Six-hour build ladder

M1 · Prove the hard path

Live Sarvam call, hardcoded Android + Web run, ugly joined report.

M2 · Compile and review

Strict four-intent schema, editable transcript, visible uncertainty.

M3 · Compare evidence

Normalized results and three guarded verdicts.

M4 · Repeat and recover

Three cases, reset, resume, and one timeout recovery.

M5 · Integrate + publish

One surface, public URL, another-device verification.

M6 · Freeze + rehearse

No features. Fallbacks, three runs, two timed demos.
